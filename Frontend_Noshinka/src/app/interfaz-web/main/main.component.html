NOSHINKA celebrates the extraordinary culinary connection between Peru and Japan — a bond forged over a century ago when Japanese immigrants brought their precision and artistry to Peru's vibrant coastal cuisine.

Our kitchen honors this legacy through every dish: the delicate balance of fresh ceviche kissed with yuzu, the umami depth of miso meeting aji amarillo.
