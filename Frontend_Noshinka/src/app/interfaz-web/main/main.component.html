Our Story

Two Cultures

NOSHINKA celebrates the extraordinary culinary connection between Peru and Japan, a bond forged more than a century ago when the Japanese came and brought their precision and artistry to the vibrant Peruvian cuisine.

Our cuisine honors this legacy in every creation: a subtle balance between freshness and acidity and the vibrant character of ingredients that converse between two cultures.
